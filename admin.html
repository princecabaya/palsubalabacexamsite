What do these event names mean?

exam_started — The exam session began. Device, screen size, visibility, and fullscreen state may be recorded.

tab_or_window_hidden — The exam page became hidden, such as switching tabs/apps or minimizing the browser. It does not identify where the student went.

tab_or_window_visible — The exam page became visible again.

window_blur — The browser window lost focus. This can happen when another app/window is tapped or when browser controls are used.

window_focus — Focus returned to the exam browser window.

fullscreen_exit — The exam left fullscreen after fullscreen had been active.

copy/cut/paste/contextmenu blocked — A restricted editing or right-click action was attempted and blocked.

keyboard/developer-tools/reload blocked — A monitored keyboard shortcut was attempted and blocked.

print_attempt / printscreen_key_detected — A detectable print or screenshot-key action occurred. Screenshot-key detection is best-effort only.

possible_speech_detected — Sustained sound consistent with possible speech was detected locally by the microphone. No audio is stored, and this event cannot identify the speaker or what was said.

student_submit_clicked — The student pressed the Submit Exam button.

exam_submitted — Supabase finalized the attempt and stored the auto-scored result.

How to interpret: Focus and visibility changes are behavioral signals only. They cannot identify the exact app, website, or reason for the change and should not be treated alone as proof of misconduct.
